THE PROBLEM & INDUSTRY CHALLENGES

Why Speech & Debate Coaching Needs AI

Traditional public speaking training is fragmented, expensive, and non-scalable.

😰

75% Speech Anxiety

Glossophobia affects 3 out of 4 individuals. Without structured, judgment-free practice environments, speakers struggle to build confidence under pressure.

⚠️

Unnoticed Logical Fallacies

Human listeners routinely fail to spot subtle cognitive fallacies (Ad Hominem, Straw Man, Slippery Slope) in fast-paced debates, weakening decision-making quality.

💸

Prohibitive Coaching Costs

Human speech coaches charge $100–$300/hr, putting personalized feedback out of reach for students, job seekers, and mid-level professionals.

📉

Lack of Quantitative Metrics

Traditional practice lacks concrete telemetry—no objective tracking of WPM pace, filler word ratios, sentence structure clarity, or skill growth over time.

Goal: Democratize access to world-class presentation and debate coaching using agentic AI.

CORE FEATURE #3

🧠 Zero-Drift NLP Fallacy Detector

Automated real-time detection of 8 key logical fallacies with credibility scoring.

Fallacy Rule Severity Pattern / Trigger Logic Correction Strategy
Ad Hominem High Attacking character/credentials instead of arguments Refocus strictly on logical premises and evidence
Straw Man High Misrepresenting/exaggerating opponent's position State opponent's position accurately before rebutting
False Dilemma Medium Forcing black-or-white choice when options exist Acknowledge nuanced middle-ground possibilities
Slippery Slope Medium Claiming small step leads to inevitable ruin Provide explicit causal proof for each step
Appeal to Authority Medium Claiming truth solely because authority said so Cite specific data, methodology, and empirical evidence
Includes Circular Reasoning, Hasty Generalization, and Red Herring detection engines with instant credibility rating (0-100%).
CORE FEATURE #4

📊 Multi-Dimensional Skill Radar Analytics

Deterministic scoring along three core performance axes.

1. Communication Score

Measures speaking rate (WPM), filler word ratio, sentence length variation, and vocal clarity.

Weight: 30% Pace + 30% Fillers + 40% Clarity

2. Logic Score

Evaluates fallacy density, premise validity, argument cohesion, and rebuttal relevance.

Weight: 100 - (Fallacy Penalty * Density)

3. Delivery Score

Assesses confidence index, time utilization, rhythm stability, and audience engagement potential.

Weight: Overall Balance Index
Visualized via interactive SVG Radar Charts tracking progress over time across multiple sessions.
